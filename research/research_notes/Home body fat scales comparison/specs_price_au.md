## Hardware specs: dimensions, weight, platform, capacity, resolution, electrodes, BIA frequency

### Takeaway
Two tiers. Foot-only, 4-electrode scales (Withings Body Smart/Comp, Garmin Index S2, Eufy P3, Xiaomi S400, Renpho basic, Tanita RD-953) cost $40-400. Handle-type, 8-electrode segmental scales (Withings Body Scan / Body Scan 2, Hume Pod, Renpho MorphoScan, Omron HBF-702T, Tanita RD-545, InBody H30) add arm/trunk/leg segment readings. Withings and Eufy give 50 g resolution and a 200 kg capacity. Garmin tops out around 181 kg and Xiaomi at 150 kg.

## Connectivity, power and ease of use

### Takeaway
Wi-Fi scales (Withings, Garmin Index S2, Eufy P3) sync without the phone present, which is the low-friction option: step on and walk away. Bluetooth-only scales (Xiaomi S400, Tanita RD-953, Omron, most Renpho) need the app open, or the phone nearby, to sync each weigh-in. For a Garmin user, Index S2 writes straight into Garmin Connect. Withings and others need a Withings to Garmin sync link.

## Metrics reported

Basic foot scales give weight, BMI, body fat %, muscle, water and bone (some add visceral fat and heart rate). Withings Body Comp adds vascular age and nerve-health features. Body Scan adds segmental, 6-lead ECG/AFib and pulse wave velocity. Body Scan 2 extends to about 60 biomarkers, with hypertension and diabetes risk features pending regulatory clearance.
