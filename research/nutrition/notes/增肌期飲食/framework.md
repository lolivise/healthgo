## 2. Framework for Darren (derived; inference labelled)

### Priority order
1. **Energy balance (positive, small).** Evidence: Rozenek 2002, Garthe 2013, Helms 2023 [U], Bray 2012 show that mass gain follows kcal and that fat gain follows excess kcal; Garthe and Helms show a bigger surplus bought extra fat but no extra lean. Inference: the "right" surplus is the smallest that still gives steady gain, since no trial shows more is better for lean mass.
2. **Total daily protein.** Morton 2018: plateau of FFM gain at ~1.62 g/kg/d total; Schoenfeld 2013: total protein was the strongest predictor. Bray: protein does not control fat gain, it raises lean share. Inference: 1.8-2.0 g/kg as margin above the 1.62 breakpoint (the protein worker owns the exact number).
3. **Fat floor.** Evidence is **weak**: Aragon 2017 says low-fat to keto all work similarly. A floor is convention (hormones, fat-soluble vitamins, palatability), not a trial result. Inference: 0.8-1.0 g/kg or ~25 % kcal.
4. **Carbohydrate = the rest.** Henselmans 2026 MA and crossover: no independent hypertrophy effect of more carb (low GRADE). Carb mainly serves training performance/appetite (convention, not tested here).
5. **Timing and frequency: minor tie-breakers.** Evidence below.

How much is evidence vs convention: steps 1-2 are evidence-backed (RCT/MA, with caveats). Step 3 is convention. Step 4 is "no evidence of harm from either choice; low-certainty null". Step 5 is mostly null.

### Meals and timing with the 8:30-12:30 entecavir window (inference)
- Evidence supports only: hit total protein, spread over roughly 3-5 feedings of 0.25-0.40 g/kg (Kerksick 2017, expert opinion; 3 vs 5 meals gave no difference in Tavares 2025, tiny n). Pre/post timing is not critical (Schoenfeld 2013). So the window costs nothing of proven value.
- Open question for Darren: his routine (bar before, shake + meal after a 60-75 min morning session) overlaps 8:30-12:30 unless training ends before 8:30. If the post-training meal falls inside the window, move the meal to 12:30; this is supported by the null post-exercise-window findings, but I did not find a trial in a morning-fasted trainee.
- Template: ~6:45 bar + train; shake before 8:30 if the session finishes in time; 12:30 large meal; ~15:30 meal; ~18:30 dinner; ~20:30 protein-rich snack (casein/dairy 20-40 g, Reis 2021, weak). 4-5 feedings, ~13 h eating span, ends before 21:00-21:30 bed. Time-restricted narrow windows cost training volume and some lean mass in 40241374, so do not compress eating to 8 h on a bulk.
- A 3,000 kcal day over 4-5 feedings = ~600-750 kcal each: bulk meals via energy-dense foods; this is practical advice, not evidence.

### Adjusting kcal by weight trend (inference)
- Use the 7-day mean weight (same conditions); compare 2-3 week windows.
- Target 0.25-0.5 %/wk (Iraki [U]) = 0.2-0.4 kg/wk at 82 kg. Slater 2019 says no validated "sweet spot", so treat this as expert opinion.
- Under 0.2 kg/wk for 2-3 weeks: add ~150-250 kcal/d (mostly carbohydrate). Over 0.5 kg/wk: remove ~150-250 kcal/d. The kcal step is my arithmetic (not from a trial; the conventional 7,700 kcal/kg value is unverified here and Slater questions the maths).
- Stop or pull back if waist/mirror fat clearly outpaces training gains; no data say what ratio to expect, and Garthe/Bray show fat gain tracks excess kcal.

## 3. Things to avoid / myths
- "Post-workout 30-60 min window": Schoenfeld 2013 MA found no adjusted timing effect (PMID 24299050).
- "Eat 6+ small meals to build more muscle": Schoenfeld 2015 MA: benefit came from one study (PMID 26024494); Tavares 2025 3 vs 5 meals null (tiny n).
- "Bigger surplus = more muscle": Garthe 2013 (LBM same, fat +15 % vs +3 %), Helms 2023 [U], Bray 2012 (fat tracks calories).
- "More carbs = more muscle": no independent effect, low-GRADE (PMID 41712097, 42356347).
- "Keto is bad for muscle in a surplus" is too strong: pooled FFM no significant difference (36231929); the one RCT (29986720) showed no lean gain on keto though. Not recommended for Darren anyway (tiny n, adherence).
- Copying bodybuilder intakes (Spendlove 2015): observational, poor quality, no outcome link; protein up to 4.3 g/kg has no demonstrated gain above ~1.6 g/kg (Morton).
- Narrow 8 h eating window on a bulk: lower volume/squat gain (40241374).
- Positional use of Roberts 2020: written for contest prep, not a bulk.

## 4. Unresolved / no good evidence
- Optimal surplus size: no trial beyond Helms 2023 [U], Garthe 2013 (elite athletes) and non-RT overfeeding (Bray); Slater says no validated number.
- Carb vs fat split at equal protein and kcal in a surplus with RT: no direct trial found.
- Fat floor in a bulk: no trial; convention only.
- Meal frequency and distribution for lean mass in a surplus: tiny, short, mixed trials only.
- Pre-sleep protein: confounded by unequal total protein (Reis 2021).
- Micronutrients/food quality for lean gain in replete young men: nothing found; vitamin D IPD-MA (patients, no benefit) does not apply.
- Thomas 2016 numbers and Slater 2019 body text (only abstracts opened).
- Rate of gain in late-novice men (Darren) and how it splits lean/fat: only the narrative guidance (Iraki [U]).
