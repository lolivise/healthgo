## Numbers for Darren (derived; inference labelled)

Assumptions (mine): body weight during the bulk ~82 kg (start 80-82, rising); protein 1.8 g/kg (range 1.6-2.2) = ~148 g = ~590 kcal; fat 0.8-1.0 g/kg (floor 0.7 per task brief) = ~66-82 g = ~590-740 kcal. Carb is the remainder, so the figures below are arithmetic, not an evidence-derived requirement.

| Calories | Protein 148 g | Fat 0.8 g/kg (66 g) | Carb remainder | g/kg | % kcal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,700 | 590 | 590 | ~1,520 kcal = ~380 g | ~4.6 | ~56% |
| 2,700 | 590 | 1.0 g/kg (82 g) 740 | ~1,370 kcal = ~340 g | ~4.2 | ~51% |
| 3,000 | 590 | 590 | ~1,820 kcal = ~455 g | ~5.5 | ~61% |
| 3,000 | 590 | 740 | ~1,670 kcal = ~420 g | ~5.1 | ~56% |

Inference: with protein and fat set, carbs land at roughly 4-5.5 g/kg (about 340-455 g) at 2,700-3,000 kcal, inside the 3-7 g/kg range quoted for RT by Escobar 2016 (PMID 27993175), but the evidence says the exact dose within that range does not matter for hypertrophy (PMID 41712097, 35215506). So choose carbs by what fills the remaining calories comfortably and by food quality.
Lower-bound inference: nothing verified here shows a minimum carb intake for hypertrophy above the keto level. Zero-carb is not needed to avoid, but keto did not beat or match higher-carb for lean gain in the surplus trial (PMID 29986720, tiny n). Keep carbs well above ~2 g/kg (arbitrary, my judgment, no verified threshold).

Carb quality (inference from Q5):
- Aim for fibre 25-29 g/d minimum (Reynolds 2019, PMID 30638909, risk reduction greatest in that range, observational + trials, not bulk-specific). Darren's 600 g vegetables plus oats plus potato/rice likely already approach it; verify with a food log (not done here).
- Whole-grain over refined is supported for general health (Reynolds), but a trial MA shows no effect of whole grain on weight/fat in RCTs overall (PMID 31390462), so do not expect body-composition benefits from it per se. Treat as health-protective, not anabolic.
- Glycaemic index: evidence is low to very low certainty (Reynolds) and one trial (PMID 39585451) showed no body-composition difference by GI in trained men. Do not use GI as a bulking rule. It may matter for liver fat but the only liver-fat GI trials are tiny/short/confounded (PMID 42044842, 36771441).
- Free sugar ~35 g/day (current): treat the sugar/fructose question as the other worker's; not assessed here beyond Hellerstein 2000 (PMID 10919929), which found no difference in fat storage between glucose, fructose and sucrose overfeeding in 4 days of a big surplus.

Timing for a 60-75 min lifting session (inference):
- Intra-session carbs are not needed. Fed vs fasted training gave the same hypertrophy and strength in 12 wk (PMID 40335157) and fed-state workouts under ~10 sets per muscle group need no extra carb (PMID 35215506). A 60-75 min session with high set count (5 days/wk, volume probably >10 sets per muscle per session on split days) is the case where a pre-workout carb-containing meal might help; the cost is trivial, so eat a normal carb meal 1-3 h before.
- Post-workout: protein is the anabolic signal; adding carbs does not raise MPS or lower MPB when protein is adequate (Staples, PMID 21131864; PMID 35182194). Carbs matter for glycogen refilling only if training twice in <8 h, which he does not.
- Carbohydrate periodisation across the day (evening vs spread) made no difference (PMID 39585451).

## Things to avoid / myths

| Claim | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "You need high carbs (6-8 g/kg) to build muscle" | MA SMD 0.15, p=0.23 (PMID 41712097); SR no benefit in 15/17 long-term trials (PMID 35215506); crossover +33 g/d carb + 485 kcal/d no effect in trained men (PMID 42356347) | Not supported. Total energy and protein do the work. Low certainty and short trials |
| "Keto is great for a bulk" | In a surplus, KD lost the lean gain advantage (PMID 29986720: muscle -0.1 vs +1.3 kg, n=9 vs 10); MA FFM -1.26 kg (PMID 33624538, mostly non-surplus trials); no FFM difference when no restriction (PMID 36231929) | Not recommended: no benefit for lean gain, plus adherence/satiety costs. Wilson 2020 (PMID 28399015) shows it need not hurt, in an isocaloric trial. Do not generalise either way (tiny n) |
| "Post-workout carbs spike insulin to boost anabolism" | PMID 21131864: insulin AUC 5x but no MPS/MPB change | Not supported when protein is adequate |
| "Fat surplus is safer for staying lean / carbs turn to fat" | Overfed fat stored 90-95% vs carb 75-85% in 14 d (PMID 7598063); similar fat balance across carb types and fat in 4 d (PMID 10919929); weight gain follows energy (PMID 32515127) | Slight edge to carb surplus for less fat storage at huge surpluses, but small and from tiny short trials. At +5-10% the difference is negligible (inference) |
| "Carb surplus is bad for liver, fat is fine" | Carb works via DNL, fat via lipid storage (PMID 33665203, narrative); SFA overfeeding raised liver fat 50% but PUFA did not (PMID 31369090) | Fat type matters as much as carb vs fat. For Hep B patient on entecavir, keep surplus modest, saturated fat low, monitor ALT/ultrasound with doctor. No trial in HBV |
| "Low-GI makes you leaner" | Low-GI trial n=7 (PMID 42044842); trained-men trial no difference (PMID 39585451); GRADE very low (PMID 30638909) | Unproven; quality of food matters more than GI number |
| "More whole grain = better body composition" | RCT MA no effect on weight/fat (PMID 31390462); one obese-adult RCT shows weight loss (PMID 41889716) | Health benefit yes (Reynolds), body-comp benefit uncertain |

## Unresolved / no good evidence

- A verified minimum carbohydrate dose for hypertrophy at high volume (5 days/wk, ~70 min). Trials are short, mostly unconstrained diets, and energy intake is a confounder (PMID 41712097 itself calls for stricter energy control).
- Keto vs higher-carb in a controlled surplus in trained people beyond one 8-wk trial with 9-10 per arm (PMID 29986720).
- Carb vs fat surplus at the small +5-10% size relevant to a lean bulk; all overfeeding calorimetry used +50% or more.
- Liver-fat response to a modest carb surplus in HBV carriers on entecavir: no trial found; extrapolation from MASLD/overweight trials only.
- Whether 35 g/day free sugar matters in a surplus (other worker's topic).
- Papers named in the brief but not located/verified: King 2022, Greene 2018, Kephart, Koopman 2007, Glynn 2010, Acheson 1988, Ashtary-Larky 2022 as a separate MA (PMID 33624538 is probably this, first author unconfirmed). Do not cite them as evidence until checked.
