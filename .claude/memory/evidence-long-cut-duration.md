description: "Is cutting too long harmful?" (2026-10-28, PubMed abstracts verified) — no study isolates duration at the same deficit; adaptation ~80–135 kcal/d appears by ~6 mo and does not grow to 2 y; it tracks fat depletion, not time; reverses in 2–12 wk; real duration costs = bone (CALERIE, no lifting) and rising disinhibition; break timing "every X weeks" = expert opinion; no plan change

**Answer:** duration on its own is barely studied. Every dataset mixes it with fat lost, leanness and deficit size, and
the severe effects (testosterone, T3, mood collapse) appear at ~4–5 % BF or ≥~1,000 kcal/d deficits, not after a set number
of weeks. All rows verified from PubMed abstracts 2026-10-28.

| Finding | Source | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| ~15 % CR 2 y: 24-h EE 80–120 kcal/d below mass-loss prediction, held to 2 y; thyroid axis down | Redman 2018 Cell Metab, PMID 29576535, CALERIE 2 n=53, chamber | good |
| 25 % CR 6 mo: adaptation ~125–135 kcal/d (~6 %), already there at 6 mo | Heilbronn 2006 JAMA, PMID 16595757, RCT n=48 overweight | fair |
| Adaptive BMR drop tracks **fat** depletion, not FFM or time | Dulloo 1998 AJCN, PMID 9734736, Minnesota re-analysis n=32 | informative, extreme |
| CALERIE 2 (~12 % CR, 2 y): −7.6 kg, FFM −2.0 (proportional); activity EE predicted FFM kept, protein didn't | Das 2017 AJCN, PMID 28228420, RCT n=218, DXA/DLW | partial (sedentary, mostly women) |
| CALERIE 2 bone: spine BMD −0.013 vs +0.007, hip −0.017 vs +0.001 g/cm²; cortisol up | Villareal 2016 JBMR, PMID 26332798 | no lifting prescribed |
| CALERIE 2 safe, well tolerated; monitor BMD, anaemia | Romashkan 2016 Oncotarget, PMID 26992237 | |
| Natural physique, ~21 wk prep: fat −5.8, FFM −1.7 kg; FT3/FT4 down, normal by 12 wk; strength kept; mood/sleep worse, better by 12 wk; ED symptoms peaked in prep; bigger post-contest kcal rise → faster RMR recovery | Buechel 2026 JISSN, PMID 42175739, cohort n=19, DXA + calorimetry | best fit |
| Bodybuilder 14.8 → 4.5 % BF: T 9.22 → 2.27 ng/mL, mood disturbance 6 → 43, both back by 6 mo; strength not fully back | Rossow 2013 IJSPP, PMID 23412685, n=1 | extremes |
| 8 mo prep: T 623 → 173, T3 123 → 40, RMR 107 → 81 % predicted; mostly reversed by month 13 | Pardue 2017 IJSNEM, PMID 28770669, n=1 | extremes |
| Ranger 8 wk (~1,000 kcal/d deficit + sleep loss): T −70 %, IGF-1 −39 %; all but T3 normal in 2–6 wk | Henning 2014 JCEM, PMID 24423293, n=23 | extremes; fast recovery |
| 62 d, 18 % deficit: those >15 % BF gained muscle | Gan 2022 JSAMS, PMID 34507882, n=17, DLW | fair |
| Continuous 6 wk vs 2 on/1 off: same RMR and body comp; disinhibition rose continuous (4.9 → 6.2), fell with breaks | Siedler 2023 J Hum Kinet, PMID 37181269, RCT n=38 trained women | fair |
| 2 refeed days/wk, 7 wk: FFM loss 0.4 vs 1.3 kg | Campbell 2020 JFMK, PMID 33467235, RCT n=27 trained | small |
| Breaks: RMR drop only with continuous, weaker in trained | Poon 2025 Nutr Rev, PMID 38193357, MA 12 RCTs n=881 | good |

- **Break timing:** no trial compares break schedules; "every 6–12 weeks" (incl. his own 10–12 wk rule) is expert opinion.
- **Unverified / not found:** CALERIE 2 mood/QoL abstract, Minnesota psychology, HRV in prep, binge risk by diet duration.

**For him (Opus, 10-28):** ~22 % BF, moderate deficit → the extreme rows don't apply; adaptation likely ≤~100 kcal/d and
reversible. Duration-linked risks that might apply: bone (lifting covers it, Villareal 2017 RT −0.5 % vs AT −3 % hip, see
[[evidence-cardio-vs-lifting]]) and creeping disinhibition (eating out, trip). His first 13 wk at low EA are the bigger
factor than week count ([[evidence-rapid-loss-harms]]). No change: the 11-08 health-guard decision in [[cutting-goal-2026]]
stands; Buechel suggests the 5.5-wk maintenance block is on the short side of full hormone recovery (6–12 wk), relevant if
the cut runs to 12-13. Related: [[evidence-metabolic-adaptation]], [[evidence-cutting]].
