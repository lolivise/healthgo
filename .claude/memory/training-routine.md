Derived from Garmin sets/activities 2026-07 → 2026-10 (first review, 2026-10-09).

- **Split, ~5 strength sessions/week, early morning:** First/Second Back Day (Sun, Wed), Chest Day
  (Sat/Tue), Shoulder Day (Tue/Sat), Leg Day (Thu), plus short "Functional Workout" sessions (11–16 min).
  About 100–110 working sets/week.
- **Cardio:** treadmill ~30 min (≈2.6–3 km) most days. It rose from 76 min/wk (W37) to 270 min/wk (W40).
- **No squat or deadlift.** Legs = leg press (180 kg × 10–12). Garmin labels are inconsistent: bench
  shows as BARBELL_BENCH_PRESS, BENCH_PRESS or UNKNOWN.
- **Reference e1RM at W40 (the cut's 12th week):** lat pulldown ~77, barbell bench ~67–73, incline DB
  ~51–55, leg press 240–252, barbell OHP 44–51 (35–40 kg × 8). All of these held steady through the cut.
- **Deliberate technique phase, from ~late Sep 2026 (W39–W40):** Darren (2026-10-09): he now focuses on
  posture and muscle feeling over load: lighter weights, slower tempo. The row dropping from 60×10 to 30×12
  is this, not strength loss. Other lifts may follow.

**How to apply:** a load drop with good recovery is **intentional**, not muscle loss. Don't call it "strength
dropping". During this phase **InBody skeletal muscle mass (SMM) is the primary muscle signal**. Use e1RM
only for lifts whose load he hasn't deliberately reduced, and use reps at the same load. Otherwise use
the reference levels above as the baseline for "is strength dropping". Training advice stays as
targeted tweaks to this split ([[review-preferences]]); watch shoulder loads ([[health-conditions]]).
