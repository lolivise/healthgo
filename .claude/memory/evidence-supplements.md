## Old belief → now
- **"Creatine damages kidneys"** → creatinine rises because creatine turns into it; measured GFR is flat (PMID 42507286). [V]
- **"Creatine causes hair loss"** → came from one 2009 rugby study of DHT [S]; first direct RCT (Lak 2025, PMID 40265319,
  n=38, 12 wk) found no change in DHT or hair. [V]
- **"More fish oil is better for the heart"** → above ~1.5 g/d in at-risk people it raises AF; benefit trials mixed.

## Contested / gaps
- Creatine eGFR: the two 2026 MAs disagree on size (−10.8 significant vs −5.2 not); both agree no injury. Want >1-yr RCTs.
- Whey: one 12-RCT MA in young adults found no lean-mass effect (PMID 41454445). Population/dose differences.
- Not found: creatine × entecavir, CoQ10 liver safety, coffee in chronic HBV, LiverTox ginseng text, COSMOS main papers.

## How it applies to Darren (2026-10-26)
Keep creatine and whey (both doing a job). Fish oil dose is in the safe cell. CoQ10, magnesium and the multivitamin
have no proven benefit for his goal; the multivitamin is the only source of ginseng, the single uncleared Hep B item.
**Dropping any of them is his or his doctor's call** (proposal given 2026-10-26); bring the full list to the next blood test.
