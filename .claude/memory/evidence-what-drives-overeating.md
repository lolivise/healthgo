Darren asked (2026-10-27, after [[evidence-inflammation-immunity-hepb]]'s chain check) whether people who eat more just
want to eat and can't control themselves. **[V]** = abstract read this session, **[U]** = summary/snippet only.

- **Willpower is a tiny factor:** impulsivity ↔ BMI r = .07 (Emery & Levine 2017 *Psychol Bull*, PMID 28493725, 142
  studies, n = 315,818, cross-sectional) [V], < 1 % of BMI variance.
- **Biology, via appetite:** BMI heritability median 0.75 twins / 0.46 families (Elks 2012, PMID 22645519, 140k twins)
  [V]; BMI loci enriched for brain pathways (Locke 2015 *Nature*, PMID 25673413, n = 339k) [V]; FTO risk allele → more
  kcal (energy-dense food), not less burn (Cecil 2008 *NEJM*, PMID 19073975, n = 2,726 kids) [V]; MC4R mutations 5.8 %
  of severe childhood obesity, hyperphagia (Farooqi 2003, PMID 12646665) [V]; satiety responsiveness 72 % heritable
  (Llewellyn 2010, PMID 20335548, infant twins) [V].
- **After loss, appetite rises:** hunger + ghrelin up, leptin/PYY down, still at 62 wk after −13.5 kg (Sumithran 2011
  *NEJM*, PMID 22029981, n = 50, no control) [V]; ≈ +100 kcal/day appetite per kg lost, > 3× the burn adaptation
  (Polidori 2016, PMID 27804272, model-derived) [V].
- **Environment moves intake with no "decision":** UPF ad lib +508 kcal/day, +0.9 vs −0.9 kg in 2 wk (Hall 2019, PMID
  31105044, n = 20) [V]; Japanese men +813 kcal (PMID 39267249, n = 9) [V]; meta of 10 RCTs: effect ≈ energy density
  (PMID 42746678, low certainty) [V]. Larger portions +295 kcal/day (PMID 34687532, 22 RCTs) [V]; lower energy density
  −223 kcal at same food weight (PMID 36460778, 38 RCTs) [V]; slower eating SMD 0.45 less (PMID 24847856) [V].
  Sleep ≤ 5.5 h +204 kcal/day (PMID 33001515, 15 studies) [V]; Al Khatib 2017 +385 [U]. Self-report under-counts
  310–820 kcal/day (PMID 33564834) [V].
- Gaps: short lab trials, mostly Western; "loss of control" eating not checked.

**For him:** his fixed home diet already removes most drivers (low energy density, set portions). Risks = eating out
