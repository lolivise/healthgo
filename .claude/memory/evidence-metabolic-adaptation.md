Darren asked (2026-10-12) when we would call metabolic adaptation and whether he'd need to "bulk".
**[V]** = checked online, **[M]** = model memory.

| Topic | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Size in moderate cuts | Br J Nutr 2022 systematic review (33 studies) [V]: AT seen in 27, small/ns in better-designed ones, fades once weight is stable. Controlled underfeeding ≈ 120 kcal/day beyond mass loss, wide spread [V]. Martins group 2020–23 [V via summary]: AT ≈ a few dozen kcal after a month of stability, not linked to regain | moderate |
| Extreme cases | Fothergill 2016 Biggest Loser [V]: −499 kcal/day AT at 6 y after −58 kg; does not transfer to a moderate cut. Natural bodybuilder case, Frontiers Nutr 2024 [V]: REE −26 %, measured/predicted 0.99 → 0.78, RHR −9 bpm, fT3 and T −37 % | low transfer |
| Biggest component | NEAT/steps; SR 36 trials, n=1,561 (Br J Nutr 2018, PMID 29845903) [V]: NEAT fell in 63 % of diet-only arms, 27 % diet+exercise, 23 % exercise-only; bigger loss ↔ bigger drop. Standing instead of sitting only +0.15 kcal/min (MA 46 studies, PMID 29385357) [V], so posture tricks are tiny; steps are the lever | moderate |
| Recovery | Physique athletes: RMR rebounds early, mostly restored within ~6 wk (Buechel 2026, abstract [V]); hormones 1–3 months (2024 review [V]) | moderate |
| Fix | Poon 2025 Nutr Rev, 12 RCTs [V]: diet breaks blunt the RMR drop (<100 kcal), same fat loss, weaker in trained people. MATADOR 2018 [V]. **No evidence a surplus is needed; maintenance is enough** [M inference] | moderate |
| Bulk | Helms 2023 Sports Med Open [V]: maintenance vs +5 % vs +15 % for 8 wk, no muscle difference, more fat with bigger surplus. Iraki 2019 [V]: +10–20 %, 0.25–0.5 %/wk. No support for bulking at 21–25 % BF [M] | moderate |

**The 6-signal rule for Darren (decided 2026-10-12).** Call meaningful adaptation only when **≥3 hold for ≥2 weeks**,
judged on fixed-intake weeks (W42 on):
1. 7-day-average weight trend < 0.1 %/wk for 3 weeks at confirmed intake (eat-out counted). Stall alone = check intake first.
2. Trend-measured TDEE < ~2,150 kcal at ~91 kg with steps unchanged (expected ~2,300–2,450).
3. Steps down ≥ 15 % from the ~9,500/day baseline (< ~8,000) without a planned reason.
4. RHR ≤ ~45 (≥5 bpm below the ~50 baseline) **with** HRV falling, or the existing RHR +5 bpm red flag.
5. Reps at the same load (or e1RM) down ≥ 5 % on ≥ 2 lifts vs his W41-onward numbers (no lift is deliberately lightened any more).
6. `/feel`: persistent fatigue, cold, hunger or worse sleep.

**Action:** ≥3 signals → bring the planned maintenance block (2026-12-14) forward, 2–4 wk at measured maintenance.
Never a bulk to fix it. Lean gain only after reaching 15–18 % BF (post-trip), +10–15 % kcal, 0.25–0.5 %/wk.
Status 2026-10-12: 0–1 of 6 (only subjective low energy); RHR 51.6 → 50.1, steps 6,488 → 9,506, HRV 37 → 43, strength flat/up.
