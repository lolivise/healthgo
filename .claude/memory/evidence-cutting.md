## Cutting without losing muscle
| Topic | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Deficit size | Murphy & Koehler 2022 (Scand J Med Sci Sports, meta-regression of RCTs) [V]: a deficit blunts lean-mass gain (ES −0.57) but not strength; deficits **> ~500 kcal/day** were associated with no lean gain. Treat 500 as a soft guide | strong |
| Rate | Garthe 2011 RCT [M]: ~0.7 %/wk kept lean mass better than ~1.4 %/wk. Helms 2014 [M]: 0.5–1 %/wk, slower as you get leaner. **Superseded for his caps by [[evidence-rapid-loss-harms]]** (0.7 %/wk, 0.5 % ≤84 kg) | moderate |
| Protein | Morton 2018 meta-analysis [M]: benefit plateaus ~1.6 g/kg (CI to 2.2). In a deficit 2.3–3.1 g/kg FFM (Helms 2014) [M]. Total daily intake matters most; 3–5 feedings. Trommelen 2023 [V]: 100 g meal still used, so no hard per-meal cap | strong |
| Fat | Whittaker & Wu 2021 meta-analysis [V via summary]: low-fat diets (~20 % energy) lower total testosterone modestly (SMD −0.38). Floor ~0.5–1 g/kg / 20–30 % energy | moderate |
| Diet breaks | MATADOR (Byrne 2018, obese men) [V]: 2 wk on / 2 wk maintenance → more loss, smaller REE drop. Peos 2021 ICECAP (lean athletes) [M] and Poon 2025 meta-analysis of 12 RCTs [V]: **no body-composition advantage**. Breaks help adherence, mood and hunger, not muscle | moderate |
| Adaptation | RMR, leptin, T3 and testosterone fall in long cuts, most in lean men; contest-prep men recover T in ~1–3 months of eating more (Frontiers Nutr 2024 review [V]). Detail in [[evidence-metabolic-adaptation]] | moderate |
| Low energy availability | IOC 2023 REDs consensus [M]: LEA ≈ <30 kcal/kg FFM/day, uncertain for men. EA = (intake − exercise kcal) ÷ FFM, not intake ÷ FFM. His floor (25 for men) is in [[evidence-rapid-loss-harms]] | strong (consensus) |
| Cardio | Schumann 2022 meta-analysis (43 studies) [V]: concurrent training does not reduce hypertrophy or max strength; small hit to explosive strength. Walking is the lowest-cost option | strong |
| Sleep | Nedeltcheva 2010 RCT (n=10) [M]: 5.5 h vs 8.5 h on the same deficit → 55 % less fat lost, 60 % more lean lost | moderate |
| When to stop | Stop signs: strength down across several lifts, persistent fatigue, worse sleep, mood, illness, or target reached. **Libido is not a signal for him** ([[feel-checkin]]) | expert |

**How to apply:**
- Keep the deficit at or under ~500 kcal/day. From 2026-10-26 he eats ≈2,520 against a TDEE of ≈2,800–3,000
  (~280–480 deficit), inside the muscle-safe zone; the W45 review (~11-08) measures TDEE properly
  ([[daily-diet-baseline]]). The first 13 weeks at ~1 %/wk were at the top end.
