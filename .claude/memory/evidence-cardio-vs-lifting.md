(2026-10-27, abstracts/full text verified) — cardio ≥ lifting for fat loss, adding RT doesn't speed it; but in a deficit cardio-only loses more lean mass + hip BMD, RT is the only mode that reliably keeps/builds it; strength and VO2 each have their own mortality link; for him keep both, no change

**Answer:** for burning fat, cardio is as good as or better than lifting. Lifting's job is the **lean mass** (and bone,
strength) that a deficit otherwise takes. They do different jobs; the best-supported plan does both.

| Finding | Source (verified 10-27) | Strength / fit |
|---|---|---|
| 8 mo, no diet: fat AT −1.66 kg, RT −0.26 (ns), AT+RT −2.44; lean RT +1.09, AT −0.10, AT+RT +0.81 | Willis 2012 J Appl Physiol, PMID 23019316, RCT n=119, DXA/BOD POD | RCT, sedentary overweight midlife; good fit |
| Deficit 500–750/d, 6 mo: fat loss equal (−6.3/−7.3/−7.0); lean AT −2.7, RT −1.0, combined −1.7; hip BMD AT −3 %, RT −0.5 %; best function = combined | Villareal 2017 NEJM, PMID 28514618, RCT n=141, DXA | RCT, older obese; poor fit |
| AT beats RT on fat mass −1.06 kg but keeps less FFM (−0.88 kg); adding RT to AT doesn't change fat loss | Lafontant 2025 JISSN, PMID 40405489, MA 36 RCTs, n=1,564 | MA, healthy adults |
| Exercise during a deficit saves +0.87 kg FFM (~46 % of the loss); mixed +1.20, strength +0.83, endurance +0.51 (ns between modes) | Deller 2026 Diabetes Obes Metab, PMID 42144246, MA 34 RCTs, n=1,455 | MA, overweight/obese |
| Young men, ~40 % deficit, RT+HIIT 6 d/wk: 2.4 g/kg protein +1.2 kg lean −4.8 kg fat vs 1.2 g/kg +0.1 / −3.5 | Longland 2016 AJCN, PMID 26817506, RCT n=40, 4-compartment | closest fit; 4 wk only |
| Cardio under-delivers vs the kcal math: 400/600 kcal × 5/wk × 10 mo → −3.9 / −5.2 kg (math ≈11–17 kg) | Donnelly 2013 Obesity, PMID 23592678, RCT n=92 | ad lib diet, age ~23 |
| At the highest dose weight loss ≈ half predicted (compensation 1.2 kg) | Church 2009 PLoS One (DREW), PMID 19223984, RCT n=411 | postmenopausal women |
| Muscle-strengthening vs none: all-cause RR 0.85; + aerobic RR 0.60; peak ~30–60 min/wk | Momma 2022 BJSM, PMID 35228201, MA 16 cohorts | observational, GRADE very low |
| Strength HR 0.77, aerobic-only 0.84, both 0.71 | Stamatakis 2018 Am J Epidemiol, PMID 29099919, cohort n=80,306 | observational, self-report |
| Low vs elite cardiorespiratory fitness HR 5.04, no upper limit | Mandsager 2018 JAMA Netw Open, PMID 30646252, n=122,007 | observational; cardio has its own value |
| Exercise in maintenance −2.81 kg regain; RT vs AT not compared | Wang 2026 Sci Rep, PMID 42331977, MA 11 RCTs | RT-specific regain benefit: no evidence found |

**For him:** in a deficit with a +3–7 kg lean goal ([[cutting-goal-2026]]), cardio-only would risk the very lean mass
the goal needs (Villareal, Deller). Treadmill stays for kcal and VO2; lifting stays for lean mass. No change to
[[training-routine]]. Hep B not studied in any of these. See [[evidence-muscle-and-metabolism]] for the "muscle burns
kcal" side.
