## Cutting without losing muscle
| Topic | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Deficit size | Murphy & Koehler 2022 (Scand J Med Sci Sports, meta-regression of RCTs) [V]: a deficit blunts lean-mass gain (ES −0.57) but not strength; deficits **> ~500 kcal/day** were associated with no lean gain. Treat 500 as a soft guide | strong |
| Rate | Garthe 2011 RCT [M]: ~0.7 %/wk kept lean mass better than ~1.4 %/wk. Helms 2014 [M]: 0.5–1 %/wk, slower as you get leaner | moderate |
| Protein | Morton 2018 meta-analysis [M]: benefit plateaus ~1.6 g/kg (CI to 2.2). In a deficit 2.3–3.1 g/kg FFM (Helms 2014) [M]. Total daily intake matters most; 3–5 feedings. Trommelen 2023 [V]: 100 g meal still used, so no hard per-meal cap | strong |
| Fat | Whittaker & Wu 2021 meta-analysis [V via summary]: low-fat diets (~20 % energy) lower total testosterone modestly (SMD −0.38). Floor ~0.5–1 g/kg / 20–30 % energy | moderate |
| Diet breaks | MATADOR (Byrne 2018, obese men) [V]: 2 wk on / 2 wk maintenance → more loss, smaller REE drop. Peos 2021 ICECAP (lean athletes) [M] and Poon 2025 meta-analysis of 12 RCTs [V]: **no body-composition advantage**. Breaks help adherence, mood and hunger, not muscle | moderate |
| Adaptation | RMR, leptin, T3 and testosterone fall in long cuts, most in lean men; contest-prep men recover T in ~1–3 months of eating more (Frontiers Nutr 2024 review [V]) | moderate |
| Low energy availability | IOC 2023 REDs consensus [M]: LEA ≈ <30 kcal/kg FFM/day, uncertain for men. EA = (intake − exercise kcal) ÷ FFM, not intake ÷ FFM | strong (consensus) |
| Cardio | Schumann 2022 meta-analysis (43 studies) [V]: concurrent training does not reduce hypertrophy or max strength; small hit to explosive strength. Walking is the lowest-cost option | strong |
| Sleep | Nedeltcheva 2010 RCT (n=10) [M]: 5.5 h vs 8.5 h on the same deficit → 55 % less fat lost, 60 % more lean lost | moderate |
| When to stop | Stop signs: strength down across several lifts, persistent fatigue, worse sleep, mood, illness, or target reached. **Libido is not a signal for him** ([[feel-checkin]]) | expert |

## Training for hypertrophy
| Topic | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Volume | Pelland 2024/25 dose-response meta-regression (67 studies) [V]: growth rises with sets on a diminishing curve; biggest gains up to ~12 sets/muscle/wk, smaller to 18–24; ~11 sets/session plateau | strong |
| Maintenance dose | Bickel 2011 [M]: 1/9 of the volume, 1 day/wk kept size for 32 wk. Spiering 2021 review [M]: ~1/3 of volume, ≥1 session/wk, keep intensity | moderate |
| Failure | Robinson 2024 Sports Med [V]: hypertrophy improves closer to failure; strength barely. Refalo 2023 [M]: failure ≈ non-failure. Default **1–2 RIR** | moderate |
| Load | Lopez 2021 [M]: ~30–85 % 1RM gives similar hypertrophy if sets are hard. Heavier is better for strength | strong |
| Tempo | Schoenfeld 2015 [M]: 0.5–8 s reps similar; >10 s reps worse. His light, slow, "feel" phase is fine **if sets end within ~0–3 reps of failure** | moderate |
| Long lengths | Wolf 2023/2025 [V], Maeo 2021/2023 [M]: stretched-position work (overhead triceps, incline curls, seated leg curl) is at least as good as full ROM | moderate |
| Age 36 | Anabolic resistance is mainly a >60 issue. No change to recommendations at 36; joint history matters more | moderate |
| Detraining | Bosquet 2013 [M]: strength largely kept 3–4 wk off; a few % lost by 5 wk, regained in 2–4 wk ("muscle memory") | moderate |
| Lean bulk (later) | Iraki 2019 [M]: +10–20 % kcal (~200–350 for him), gain 0.25–0.5 %/wk, 8–16 wk; ~⅓ of the gain is fat. Recomposition in trained men is slow (Barakat 2020) [M] | weak–moderate |
| Shoulder | No trials for OHP pain. Practice: landmine press, neutral-grip DB press, scapular-plane raises, keep pain ≤3/10, physio for recurring flares | weak |

**How to apply:**
- In a deficit, keep the volume he has (~10–16+ hard sets/muscle/wk). If recovery drops, cut sets, not intensity.
- Treat his 1,975–2,075 kcal with ~2,330 maintenance (~250–350 deficit) as inside the muscle-safe zone. The first
  12 weeks at ~1 %/wk were at the top end.
- Holiday minimum: 1–2 short full-body sessions/week near failure hold muscle. 5 weeks of light training costs little.
