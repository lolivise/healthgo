**Answer:** the clock shift is real and measured; "hormonal imbalance" from 1–2 shifted days a week is not shown.
No study defines a safe size of shift. Record: `research/claims/2026-11-01-sleep-timing-social-jetlag.md`.

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Weekend ~1.5 h later bed + ~3 h later wake → DLMO −45 min; back to baseline by next Friday; ~1 h later wake (nap) still −41 min | Crowley 2010 Chronobiol Int, PMID 20795887, adolescents n=12/33 |
| Same bedtime, wake 3 h later daily for 2 wk → DLMO −2.4 h (late wake alone shifts the clock, via missed morning light) | Burgess & Eastman 2006, PMID 16309837, n=14 |
| ~12 h misalignment → leptin −17 %, insulin +22 %, glucose +6 %, cortisol rhythm reversed (shift-work scale, not weekends) | Scheer 2009 PNAS, PMID 19255424, n=10 |
| Social jetlag vs none: BMI +0.49, HbA1c +0.42 %, no link to lipids / HOMA-IR; I² up to 100 %, confounded by chronotype | MA 68 obs. studies, PMID 36351658 (2023) |
| Social jetlag ≥2 h vs <1 h: metabolic syndrome OR 1.52 — the only threshold-like number; categories are convention | MA, PMID 39158856 (2024), 2–3 studies |
| Weekend catch-up doesn't undo weekday 5 h restriction (insulin sensitivity, clock delay) | Depner 2019 Curr Biol, n=36 |
| Short weekday + long weekend sleep: no excess mortality vs 7 h (<65 y) | Åkerstedt 2019, PMID 29790200, n=43,880 cohort |
| 13–14 h single nights: never studied; habitual ≥9 h ↔ mortality RR 1.39 (reverse causation) | PMID 28890167, 137 cohorts |
| Exercise timing shifts the clock: 7 am / 1–4 pm advance, 7–10 pm delay | Youngstedt 2019 J Physiol, PMID 30784068, n≈100 |
| Training doesn't raise resting testosterone: SMD 0.00 (−0.20 to 0.20) | MA 11 RCTs, PMID 35134000, n=421 men |

**Unverified, not to cite:** "the clock re-aligns ~1 h/day"; morning light preventing the weekend delay (Crowley's 1 h
blue light didn't; camping studies Wright 2013 / Stothard 2017 n=8/14 via web only). No study compares late bed vs
late wake at equal sleep length.

**Him (Garmin 2026-09-06 → 11-01, 57 nights):** workdays 23:44 → 06:18 (6.6 h), weekends 01:29 → 08:33; social
jetlag **+2.0 h**, mid-sleep SD 90 min, 10 nights >2 h late (all weekends/late nights). Early-morning training already
sits in the advancing window.

**For his plan:** no change. Proposals for `/review`: weekday sleep ≥7 h stays the main lever ([[safety-limits]]);
on weekends pull bedtime back first, wake ≤~2 h later than weekdays, daylight after waking (direction from the
experiments, number not trial-tested). Melatonin = new supplement → doctor first ([[health-conditions]]).
