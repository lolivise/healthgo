Darren saw a YouTuber (2026-10-25) say a Japanese vascular doctor, **池谷敏郎 (Ikeya Toshiro)**, claims working out ages
you faster. Full 繁中 report: `research/aging/運動會讓人老得快嗎 池谷敏郎 血管.md`. **[V]** read online, **[U]** secondary.

- **池谷 never wrote it.** His books say ROS harm vessels (causes listed: stress, UV, smoking, age, glucose spikes,
  not exercise) and "運動 brings good news" for the antioxidant enzymes that decline with age [V]. He says intense
  exercise is *unnecessary*, recommends walking + light RT + 4–5 min post-meal "ゾンビ体操" (no study cited). He lifts
  himself. The YouTube claim is most likely a clipped "激しい運動は不要" + "活性酸素". Exact video not seen: ask for
  the link if he wants the quote checked.
- **The real Japanese vessel study: Miyachi 2004, *Circulation*** (full text read [V]): 28 sedentary men aged 22,
  80 % 1RM, last set to failure, 3×/wk, 4 months, **no aerobic** → carotid compliance −19 %, β-stiffness +21 % within
  2 months; **fully reversed after 4 months detraining**; BP, IMT, femoral artery unchanged. Authors: protocol far above
  guidelines, "properly prescribed" RT still encouraged.
- Follow-ups: Miyachi 2013 meta, PMID 22267567 [V 10-28] (8 RCTs, n=193): RT +10.7 % stiffness overall (I² 89 %); high intensity +11.6 % (all ages), moderate none; young +14.3 %, middle-aged no change;
  Kawano 2006, PMID 16915024 [V] (n=39 young men, 4 mo): **adding aerobic prevents the stiffening** (RT alone ≈ −20 % carotid compliance, reversed on detraining; `tiny n`, `surrogate`); Zhang 2021 meta (20 studies): RT overall null, low–mod
  intensity lowers PWV, high intensity NS [V]; Shaw 2026 (≥60 y): RT neutral, aerobic −0.62, combined −0.85 m/s [V].
- **ROS is the signal:** Ristow 2009 (~10/group, 4 wk, C 1,000 mg + E 400 IU): blocked exercise's insulin-sensitivity
  and antioxidant-enzyme gains (mitohormesis). ⚠️ **Outcome blunting is NOT established:** Yfanti 2010 (same doses, 12 wk)
  no blunting [U]; Clifford 2020 meta (18 RCTs) no effect on VO2max, endurance, lean mass, strength [V]; only elderly
  men (Bjørnsen 2016, PMID 26129928 [V], RCT n=34, 60–81 y) gained less lean mass (+1.4 vs +3.9 %, P=0.04; strength unaffected). Signalling blunting is real, functional harm unproven. His stack
  has no listed C/E; high-dose antioxidants are already barred by [[supplements]]; food sources are fine.
- **Outcomes:** Momma 2022 meta: RT all-cause −15 %, **RT + aerobic −40 %**; J-curve, lowest at 40 min/wk (CVD 60),
  point estimate back to 1 at ~130–140 min/wk, very low certainty, self-report [V]. Lee 2022: aerobic 2–4× guideline,
  no harm. Elite athletes SMR 0.67. Master@Heart 2023: lifelong endurance → more but more stable plaque. Werner 2019:
  endurance/HIIT lengthen telomeres, RT neutral.
- **Upper dose / "intense exercise hurts the heart" (/ask 2026-10-27, all observational) [V abstracts]:** mortality curve
  plateaus, no rise (SR 41579290: U-shape evidence "limited"; MA 40487062: vigorous 180 min/wk −23 % CVD death).
  Running: no pace/volume dose-response (MA 31685526, HR CVD 0.70); Schnohr 2015 fast-jogger U = tiny n, all-cause.
  Real signals only at extreme endurance: **AF** rises beyond ~78 MET-h/wk (MA 42861574, athletes OR 2.74); **CAC** in men
  >3,000 MET-min/wk (MA 40373527) but mostly calcified, events equal or lower (SR 42007192). Acute exertion MI trigger
  mostly in the sedentary (Mittleman 1993). No RT→MI/CAD evidence. **He is far below all thresholds; nothing changes.**
  Palpitations/irregular HR or chest pain in exercise → doctor.
- **Applied to him:** RT 250–310 min/wk (Garmin, incl. rest), mostly 8–20 reps; treadmill 220–490 min/wk at HR
  118–140 → he is in Kawano's "aerobic cancels it" group and Momma's best (combined) group. **Plan unchanged.** During
  future bulk cycles: **never cut cardio to add lifting**, and no need to add more RT time. One habit: **exhale on
  effort, no long breath-holds** (heavy lifts spike BP to ~320/250 mean, one >480/350: MacDougall 1985, PMID 3980383 [V], n=5 bodybuilders, intra-arterial line), esp. leg press. No new tracking; BP at the GP visit.
- **Breath-hold (Valsalva, /ask 2026-10-27) [V abstracts]:** acute only, healthy young, n 11–44: VM +37 mmHg MAP and a
  transient PWV rise vs paced breathing (42213131); peak MAP + cerebral pulsatility up at 90 % 1RM leg press, blunted in
  trained (42841366); biggest cerebral swing *after* the hold (31912226, → stand up slowly). IAP: squat >200, leg press
  ~160–176, bench 79 mmHg (SR 31938009). **No outcome evidence of harm in healthy lifters**; events = case reports, mostly
  undiagnosed aortic disease / hypertension / stimulants. Rule: **1–2 s brace on the hard rep at most, never across
  reps**; heavy breathing after a set is normal. Thunderclap headache, tearing chest/back pain, fainting → ED.
