Darren asked (2026-10-26) whether 糖油混合物 (sugar+fat foods) and carb+protein combinations are bad, and why.
**[V]** = abstract checked, **[S]** = memory/secondary. Flags name what is weak.

## Old belief → now
**"Insulin makes fat stick, so carb+fat (or carb+protein) together is fattening"** → at matched kcal the macro mix barely
matters; lower-fat isocaloric diets lose slightly *more* fat (Hall 2017 PMID 28193517, 32 feeding studies; Hall 2015
PMID 26278052, n=19) [V]. **But "mixing doesn't matter" is wrong too:** fat+carb foods are valued and eaten *more* — the
harm is overeating, not a toxic reaction.

## Sugar + fat (糖油混合物)
| Question | Answer | Key source | Flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reward | supra-additive in humans: fat+carb bid higher, more striatal response than either alone at equal kcal | DiFeliceantonio 2018 Cell Metab (PMID 29909968) [V] | valuation, not intake; n≈20s; not replicated |
| Why (mouse) | separate vagal fat and sugar circuits add up on dopamine → overeating | Cell Metab 2024 (PMID 38242133) [V] | **mouse only** — the source of most Chinese-media claims |
| Definition | hyper-palatable (HPF) fat+sugar = >20 % kcal fat AND >20 % kcal sugar; 62 % of US foods qualify | Fazzino 2019 (PMID 31689013) [V] | classification, not a causal test |
| Intake | more HPF share → more kcal per meal; energy density + eating rate + HPF each predict intake | Fazzino 2025 n=29 (PMID 40288138); Hall data 2,733 meals (PMID 37117850) [V] | small, observational |
| Ultra-processed (UPF) | +508 kcal/day ad lib, +0.9 kg/2 wk; UK 8-wk replication: −1.05 % vs −2.06 % weight | Hall 2019 n=20 (PMID 31105044); Dicken 2025 Nat Med n=55 (PMID 40760353) [V] | **ad lib only**; sugar+fat not isolated; modest effect |
| Equal kcal | **no human trial** shows the combo is more fattening than the same kcal apart | — | gap |
| Liver | +1,000 kcal/day 3 wk: liver fat sat fat +55 %, sugar +33 %, unsat fat +15 %; sat fat also ↑ insulin resistance, ceramides | Luukkonen 2018 (PMID 29844096) [V] | overfeeding, surrogate, no combo arm |
| AGEs / acrylamide | high-AGE diets raise markers only; acrylamide–cancer null in MAs | PMID 26938557, 32077494 [V] | markers; acrylamide comes from browned starch, not the combo |
| Brain / gut | human data correlational; damage shown in rodents | PMID 34400179 [V] | animal-led |

## Carb + protein and other "bad combos" (食物相剋 / 分食法)
| Claim | Verdict | Key source | Flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food combining (Hay diet) beats balanced | **myth** | Golay 2000 RCT n=54, 6 wk, equal kcal: −6.2 vs −7.5 kg, NS (PMID 10805507) [V] | only RCT; small |
| Protein+starch "rot", pH conflict | **myth** | stomach + pancreas secrete proteases, amylase, lipase together [textbook GI physiology; no food-combining trial] | no human data |
| Veg → meat → rice lowers glucose | helpful, small | PATTERN, healthy Chinese adults, rice/chicken/veg: ↓ glucose, ↑ GLP-1, no extra insulin (PMID 31053510) [V]; Shukla 2017 T2D −53 % iAUC (PMID 28989726) [V] | n=16, single meals, no fat-loss outcome |
| Carbs add muscle growth to protein post-lift | **myth** for MPS; real for glycogen | Glynn 2010 n=13 (PMID 20519362) [V]; Staples 2011 n=9 crossover, 25 g whey ± 50 g maltodextrin, no added MPS (PMID 21131864) [V] | biopsy, `tiny n` |
| 豆漿+雞蛋, 牛奶+柳丁, 海鮮+維C (arsenic) | **myth** | soy trypsin inhibitor is heat-labile (PMID 25678063 [V abstract], lab: Kunitz inactivates fast, Bowman-Birk slowly; no residual %); no human harm data found | — |
| 菠菜+豆腐 → stones | **myth, reversed** | calcium binds oxalate in the gut → *less* absorbed (PMID 30566003) [V] | — |
| Tea/coffee/cocoa ↓ non-haem iron | **real**, matters only if iron-short | −50–94 % per meal (Hurrell 1999, PMID 10999016) [V] | single meal |
| Vit C + iron, fat + carotenoids | helpful | PMID 15277161 (n=7) [V] | tiny n |

## Hype spotted
"Sugar+oil is toxic/addictive" (mouse data stretched to humans; food addiction disputed); "uniquely fattening at equal
kcal" (never tested); "insulin makes fat stick" (contradicted); Hay-diet pH/putrefaction (no physiology); "carbs last
burns fat" (glucose surrogate, T2D, no weight outcome); 食物相剋 lists mixing one real mineral effect with invented poisonings.
