**Reported 2026-10-13.** None of this happened in his 10 years in Australia before the cut (cut started 2026-07-15):
- backs of hands and fingertips dry for ~2 months, now **peeling**;
- **itchy back, shoulders and neck**;
- the eye-area cream he has used without trouble now often **stings/irritates**, and the area itches without it too.

**Assessment (Opus, 2026-10-13, not a diagnosis):**
1. **Diet: one real gap, linoleic acid (LA, omega-6).** His fats are olive oil (mostly oleic), fish oil (EPA/DHA) and
   chia (ALA). Estimated LA ≈ **6–7 g/day** (chicken ~1.8, eggs ~1.6, olive oil 14 g ~1.4, oats ~0.8, chia ~0.6, bar ~0.5);
   it was lower before the 2026-10-09 oil increase. The AU/NZ NRV adequate intake for men is **13 g/day**. LA is the
   fatty acid the skin barrier (ceramides) needs; fish oil does not replace it. Overt EFA deficiency (dry, scaly,
   peeling skin) is documented only with very low-fat diets / TPN [V: BMJ 1975 case, Riella 1975 TPN series], so his
   intake (~3 % of energy) is **suboptimal, not deficient**. Plausible contributor, not proven cause.
   Other nutrients are covered: zinc (multi 15 mg + chicken), vitamin A (carrot, spinach, kale), B vitamins, protein
   ~1.9 g/kg, water 3.5–4 L.
2. **A long deficit itself:** ~13 weeks at ~1 %/wk lowers sebum and thyroid output (T3), both of which dry skin
   ([[evidence-cut-and-training]], Adaptation row). Human data directly on weight loss and skin barrier is thin
   (2024 Frontiers Nutr study was mice, no barrier loss).
3. **Non-diet causes, probably the biggest:** daily treadmill + gym → more sweat and showers (hot water and soap strip
   lipids), meal-prep / dish / café hand washing, Perth spring wind and low humidity. Itch on back/neck/shoulders fits
   sweat + shower xerosis.
4. **⚠️ Hep B:** itch (pruritus) is a recognised symptom of chronic liver disease; reported in ~8–35 % of chronic HBV
   patients, more in the immune-active phase [V: US cohort 35 %; Central European study 24 %]. Bloodwork has been normal,
   so it's unlikely, but it **must go to his doctor**, not be explained away by diet ([[health-conditions]]).
