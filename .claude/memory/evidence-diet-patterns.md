## Verdict per diet
| Diet | Calorie-matched result | Key evidence | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **16:8 TRE** | = CR. Works only by eating ~400 kcal less without counting | Liu 2022 NEJM, TRE+CR vs CR 12 mo, −1.8 kg ns (35443107) [V]; Lin 2023, TRE ≈ 25 % CR (37364268) [V]; MA 20 RCTs: +TRE −0.9 kg, FFM −0.56 (41034862) [V]; Lowe 2020 lean-mass index worse (32986097) [V] `tiny n` | people who find skipping a meal easier than counting |
| TRE + lifting | lean mass held, no edge | Moro 2016, trained men at maintenance: FFM, strength held, **testosterone and IGF-1 fell** (27737674) [V] `tiny n`; MA 8 RCTs FFM ns (41687432) [V] | as above |
| **20:4 / one meal a day** | no trial shows it better | Stote 2007, 1 vs 3 meals: **BP and LDL up**, hunger up (17413096) [V] `tiny n`; Tinsley 2017 (4 days/wk only) lean gain favoured normal eating (27550719) [V]; Areta 2013: 4×20 g beats 2×40 g for MPS (23459753) [V] `surrogate` | almost nobody; not lifters chasing protein |
| **3–7-day fast** | never compared vs matched CR | Laurens 2021, 10-day fast: **~60 % of loss lean soft tissue**, BMR −12 %, lean still −2–3 % after refeed (34668663) [V] `tiny n`; Buchinger n=1,422 safe but 200–250 kcal, clinic-run (30601864) [V] `industry` | supervised clinics only; no proven general use |
| **Keto** | no fat-loss edge; ward study fat loss *slowed* | Hall 2016 ward (27385608) [V]; DIETFITS 12 mo 0.7 kg ns (29466592) [V]; Cochrane low-carb 1–2 yr −0.9 kg (35088407) [V]; lifters: FFM −1.3 kg vs non-keto MA (33624538) [V] `hetero`; Greene 2018 lean −2.3 kg (30335720) [V]; LDL +8 mg/dL avg, wide spread (42056882) [V] | drug-resistant child epilepsy (Cochrane 32588435) [V]; T2D short-term glucose under a doctor (33441384) [V] |
| **Small frequent meals** | no metabolic boost | Cameron 2010, 3 vs 6 meals same deficit: no difference (19943985) [V]; NMA 22 RCTs: 2 meals ≥ 3 or 6 (32437566) [V]; TEF follows total intake [S] | lifters spreading protein (3–5 feedings); big-hunger people |

## Hype spotted
- **"Fasting = autophagy / cell cleanup"**: no human tissue measurement after a fast; the one human muscle study saw no
  rise (35660501) [V]. Rodent data. Human RCT protocols (39770918) admit the gap.
- **"Fasting regenerates the immune system"**: Cheng 2014 was mice (24905167) [V].
- **Fasting-mimicking diet**: Longo's trial (28202779) `surrogate` `short`; L-Nutra (ProLon maker) sponsors the follow-ups (41372565) `industry`.
- **"Keto burns more fat"**: early scale drop is water + glycogen; the ward study saw slower fat loss.
- **"6 small meals boost metabolism"**: no trial supports it.
- **"8-h eating window → 91 % higher CV death"** (AHA 2024): conference abstract, never peer-reviewed, window from 2 days
  of recall, reverse causation `observational` `abstract≠data`. The peer-reviewed NHANES follow-up (40944446) has the same flaws.
- **"High LDL on keto is harmless"** (KETO-CTA 39372369, 40192608): self-selected, no baseline, plaque still progressed.

## Hep B
Mouse: fasting → PGC-1α → **more HBV transcription**, reversed by refeeding (Shlomai 2006, 17043229) [V] `animal`.
No human data on fasting in treated HBV (Ramadan studies excluded HBV). Unknown, not proven risk. Any multi-day fast = ask the doctor.
