## Training for hypertrophy
| Topic | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Volume | Pelland 2024/25 dose-response meta-regression (67 studies) [V]: growth rises with sets on a diminishing curve; biggest gains up to ~12 sets/muscle/wk, smaller to 18–24; ~11 sets/session plateau | strong |
| Frequency | Schoenfeld 2019 (25 studies, PMID 30558493) [V]: volume-equated, frequency makes no meaningful difference to hypertrophy, trained or not. Pelland 2025 (PMID 41343037) [V]: frequency effect on hypertrophy compatible with negligible; on strength real, diminishing. Split vs full-body: Ramos-Campo 2024 JSCR meta, 14 studies, n=392 (PMID 38595233) [V abstract]: volume-equated, no difference in strength or hypertrophy (wide CIs, k=4–6 for size) → layout is preference | strong |
| Maintenance dose | Bickel 2011 MSSE, PMID 21131862 [V 10-28] (RCT n=70, 16 wk training then 32 wk at 1/3 or 1/9 dose, biopsies + MRI): **1/9 of the dose kept size in 20–35 y**, not in 60–75 y; not in a deficit, only 16 wk trained. Spiering 2021 JSCR, PMID 33629972 [V] (narrative review): younger people keep strength and size up to 32 wk on **1 session/wk, 1 set per exercise, if relative load is kept**; older need 2/wk, 2–3 sets. (The "1/3" is Bickel's, not Spiering's.) Fits the trip, which is at maintenance, not a deficit | moderate |
| Failure | Robinson 2024 Sports Med [V]: hypertrophy improves closer to failure; strength barely. Refalo 2023, PMID 36334240 [V] ([[evidence-load-reps-stretch]]): failure ≈ non-failure. Default **1–2 RIR** | moderate |
| Load | Lopez 2021, PMID 33433148 [V 10-28] (network MA, 28 studies, n=747, all sets to failure): hypertrophy load-independent from >15RM to ≤8RM; strength: high and moderate beat low (SMD 0.60 / 0.34), high vs moderate ns. (The "30–85 % 1RM" range is not in the abstract; it uses RM bands.) | moderate–strong |
| Tempo | Schoenfeld 2015, PMID 25601394 [V] (MA, 8 studies, sets to failure): 0.5–8 s reps similar; >10 s looks worse, few studies. His light, slow, "feel" phase is fine **if sets end within ~0–3 reps of failure** | moderate |
| Long lengths | Wolf 2023/2025 [V]; Maeo 2021, PMID 33009197 [V] (seated > prone leg curl, hamstrings +14 vs +9 %); Maeo 2023, PMID 35819335 [V] (overhead > neutral triceps extension, n=21, `tiny n`): stretched-position work (overhead triceps, incline curls, seated leg curl) is at least as good as full ROM | moderate |
| Age 36 | Anabolic resistance is mainly a >60 issue. No change to recommendations at 36; joint history matters more | moderate |
| Detraining | Bosquet 2013 Scand J Med Sci Sports, PMID 23347054 [V abstract 10-28] (MA 103 studies): full cessation lowers max force SMD −0.46, submax strength −0.62, power −0.20; grows with time off, worse >65 y and in inactive people. **The "3–4 wk kept / 2–4 wk to regain" figures are not in the abstract: unverified** | moderate |
| Lean bulk (later) | Iraki 2019 Sports, PMID 31247944 [V] (narrative review, expert opinion): +10–20 % kcal (~200–350 for him), 0.25–0.5 %/wk for novice/intermediate, more conservative when advanced. Only RCT: Helms 2023, PMID 37914977 [V] (n=17, 8 wk): +15 % added skinfolds, not muscle thickness, vs maintenance → favour the low end. "~⅓ of the gain is fat" unverified. Barakat 2020 Strength Cond J (no PMID, abstract via Crossref): recomposition in trained people is well documented; "slow" is not in the abstract. See [[evidence-body-fat-and-muscle-growth]] | weak–moderate |
| Shoulder | No trials for OHP pain. Practice: landmine press, neutral-grip DB press, scapular-plane raises, keep pain ≤3/10, physio for recurring flares | weak |

**How to apply:**
- In a deficit, keep the volume he has (~10–16+ hard sets/muscle/wk). If recovery drops, cut sets, not intensity.
- **Bulk with 4 lifting days (asked 10-26):** fine for hypertrophy if weekly hard sets per muscle stay the same. Lift
  on WFH days + weekend, walk on the 3 office days. Move the dropped day's sets into the other 4, keep any one muscle
  ≤~11 hard sets per session, keep the shoulder's 72 h gap. Cost: a little strength progress, ~300 kcal/wk less burn
  (fold into the surplus by weight trend). Not decided as a schedule yet; set it when the bulk is planned.
- **Halving back to ~10 sets/wk, once a week (asked 10-26; he kept Wednesday back after all):** defensible. Schoenfeld 2017 (PMID 27433992):
  graded dose-response, ~+0.37 % growth per extra weekly set, so 20 → 10 sets costs roughly 3–4 percentage points of
  growth over a block; Schoenfeld 2019 trained men (PMID 30153194): more volume = more size, not more strength; most of
  the gain sits below ~12 sets (Pelland). 10 sets in one session is under the ~11-set plateau. In the cut, growth isn't
  the aim and 10 sets holds muscle easily. Adherence: pleasant affect during exercise predicts future activity
  (Rhodes & Kates 2015, PMID 25921307, 24 studies).
- Holiday minimum: 1–2 short full-body sessions/week near failure hold muscle. 5 weeks of light training costs little.
