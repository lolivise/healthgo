Darren, 2026-10-20: "My main goal is to keep my body in good shape. During my diet, I should look forward to my
physical performance or how well I perform in sports."

**Why:** he wants to look and feel fit, not just weigh less ([[user-profile]], [[cutting-goal-2026]]). With no InBody
any more ([[data-sources]]), performance is also the only real check that the weight lost is fat, not muscle.

**How to apply:**
- In every review, judge the cut **performance first**, scale second. Put a short performance line in the verdict
  block: strength vs the W41+ baseline ([[training-routine]]), treadmill HR at the same pace, and RHR/HRV/sleep.
- A falling scale with holding or rising performance = on track, even near the top of the rate limit.
- **Performance dropping is a reason to slow the cut on its own**, before any [[safety-limits]] red flag fires: a real
  e1RM drop (>5 % on a main lift vs its W41+ best, two sessions running) or the same treadmill pace costing a clearly
  higher HR → add back ~150–200 kcal (fat first, since he is near the fat floor) rather than push on.
- Never trade performance for a faster scale drop.
