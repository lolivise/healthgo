Darren asked (2026-10-25) to validate every anti-aging point in 池谷敏郎's books (vascular-age doctor, see
[[evidence-exercise-and-aging]]). Full 繁中 table: `research/reports/池谷敏郎 抗老建議 逐條查證.md`. Sources were free
book excerpts (PRESIDENT Online, 東洋経済, edh.tw), so the claim list is partial. [U] = snippet only.

**Overall:** direction right (lose visceral fat, low salt, no alcohol/smoking, walk, coffee, green tea, fish, olive
oil), numbers overstated, his own figures (vessel age 28, 10 % BF) self-reported.

**Supported, useful:**
- Amount over macro ratio (Hall & Guo 2017: ~26 kcal/d difference); strict low-carb no better after 12 mo (Silverii 2020).
- Eating carbs last: −53 % glucose iAUC in T2D (Shukla 2017); healthy-adult data thin.
- Same meal later in the day → higher glucose; "dinner can be anything" is wrong.
- Coffee 3–4 cups: all-cause RR 0.83 (Poole 2017). Green tea 3–4 cups: HR ~0.87 (JPHC, Saito 2015). Alcohol: no protective dose.
- Salt: SSaSS 2021 stroke −14 %, death −12 %; his 8 g is the old Japan DRI, WHO <5 g.
- **Warm bath/shower 40–42.5 °C, 1–2 h before bed, ≥10 min → faster sleep onset, better efficiency** (Haghayegh 2019 meta).
- Sitting: ≥35.5 MET-h/wk removes the excess risk of >8 h/day sitting (Ekelund 2016). He is far above it.
- Smoking cessation figures (Doll 2004, Jha 2013); dog ownership (confounded); gait speed (elderly marker).

**Wrong or overstated:** sugar "more addictive than drugs" (rat data; Westwater 2016 says not compelling); coffee
treats asthma; NAFLD worse than alcoholic liver disease (opposite, Marot 2017); fatty liver doubles MI risk (it is
OR 1.64, Targher 2016); muscle is "the only" way to raise metabolism (only ~13 kcal/kg/day, Wang 2010); slouching =
looking 40 y older; "vessel age" numbers (baPWV/CAVI predict CVD, but age conversion is marketing).
**Untested:** zombie exercise (4–5 min = 10 min walk), in-bed pre-sleep exercise, trigonelline beige fat,
folate/aspiration, beta-cell exhaustion, amazake/black-bean yogurt, 1-min post-meal move (only longer breaks tested).

**His supplements, all off-limits for him** ([[supplements]], Hep B rule): NMN (small 12-wk RCTs, no liver data),
hydrogen tablets (limited), long-acting vit C (high dose can raise ALT [U]), 玄米酵素 (no trials), prescription EPA (AF
3.1 vs 2.1 % in REDUCE-IT; >1 g/d AF HR 1.49 [U]) → **don't raise his 1.2 g fish oil**. Green tea *extract* toxic ≥800 mg EGCG.

**Applied to him (2026-10-25):** his diet already matches the supported parts (carbs at breakfast/lunch, dinner =
yogurt + oats, fixed kcal, 1.9 g/kg protein > 1.62 plateau, olive oil ↑, coffee, no alcohol/smoking).
✅ **Adopted by Darren 2026-10-25:** (1) 40–42 °C bath/shower ≥10 min, 1–2 h before bed (sleep is his weakest
metric; judged at the first review ≥ 11-08, see [[review-preferences]]); (2) eating order veg → chicken → rice at the
rice meals ([[daily-diet-baseline]]). Not adopted: (3) post-meal walk.
Optional food swap: fish 1–2×/wk for one chicken meal (he eats no fish).
