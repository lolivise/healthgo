## Old belief → now
**"The lipid panel (TG, TC, HDL, LDL) tells you your cholesterol risk"** → it misses Lp(a), a genetic, causal ASCVD
risk factor; calculated LDL-C even includes Lp(a)-cholesterol. Measure Lp(a) once (EAS 2022, PMID 36036785 [V]).

## Evidence
| Question | Answer | Key source | Flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who tests Lp(a) | **every adult once**; aim = find ≥180 mg/dL (~top 5–10 %, FH-level lifetime risk) | EAS consensus Kronenberg 2022 Eur Heart J, PMID 36036785 [V]; Brussels 2025 (40340180), SISA 2023 (37586921) agree; not every body says universal | consensus, MR + cohorts; no RCT shows testing improves outcomes |
| Cut-offs | <30 mg/dL (<75 nmol/L) rule-out, >50 (>125 nmol/L) rule-in, 30–50 grey; risk continuous, ~100 mg/dL ≈ 2× ASCVD; MI rises from ~75th centile, ischaemic stroke/CV death only >95th | EAS 2022 full text [V] | mg/dL ↔ nmol/L conversion approximate; lab units vary |
| Can it be changed | >90 % genetic (LPA locus). Diet ±10–15 %: lower SFA *raises* it (SMD 0.14, MA 27 RCTs n=1,325, PMID 38964657 [V]); exercise −2.5 mg/dL (MA 25 RCTs, PMID 40203891 [V]). **Liver disease lowers it, kidney disease raises it** (review PMID 35606076 [V]) | | small effects |
| If high | tighter LDL-C/BP control, family cascade testing, no niacin, no routine aspirin. No Lp(a) drug with outcome benefit: pelacarsen Lp(a)HORIZON (n≈8,300, CVD) missed its primary MACE endpoint, 2026-09 topline | EAS 2022 [V]; HORIZON = press release only [S] | full data pending |
| oxLDL | association only: highest vs lowest RR 1.79 (1.56–2.05), MA 12 observational (Gao 2017, PMID 29173602 [V]); **no standard range** (assays/units differ; Mercodia kit range 26–117 vs a UK lab's 14.4–102.7 mU/L); no guideline recommends it; no RCT of lowering it; statin–oxLDL MA PMID 35958018 **retracted** | | marker, not causal; "functional ideal <115" unsourced |
| Trans fat | industrial TFA per 1 % energy replacing cis-MUFA: LDL/HDL ratio +0.055 (0.044–0.066); ruminant similar (Brouwer 2010 PLoS One, PMID 20209147 [V], 39 feeding trials) | | lipid marker; blood TFA can't name a food; grilling isn't a TFA source [S] |
| Deficit and blood counts | Cytopenia/marrow atrophy is an **anorexia-level** finding (~1/3 anaemia, reverses on refeeding; Hütter 2009 review, PMID 19040272 [V]). Moderate CR (CALERIE 2, n=218, ~12 % CR, 2 y): Hb/Hct/iron unchanged (PMID 41825741 [V], both arms on an 18 mg iron multivitamin); WBC/lymphocytes slightly lower but in range, vaccine response and infections unchanged (Meydani 2016, PMID 27410480 [V]) | | CALERIE non-lifters |

## How it applies to Darren (2026-11-04)
- **Lp(a): proposed** — ask the GP to add it once at the next routine blood test (cost/Medicare not checked). Not known
  whether he has had it. With Hep B the GP interprets it (liver disease can make it read low). A high value changes his
  long-term LDL target, not the cut. Next to LDL/apoB from [[evidence-eggs-and-cholesterol]].
- **oxLDL: don't buy it.** LDL/apoB are the causal markers.
- **Blood counts:** his cut (EA ~26–27 kcal/kg, ~400 kcal deficit) is CALERIE-like, so no anaemia expected. Any low count
  goes to the doctor, never self-attributed to the diet (Hep B, [[health-conditions]]). No iron or other supplement.
