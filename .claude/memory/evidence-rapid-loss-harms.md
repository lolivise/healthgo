Darren asked (2026-10-25) how to define cutting too fast and its side effects. Full 繁中 report with links:
`research/medical/慢性發炎 減重過快 臉部老化.md`; notes in `research/medical/notes/慢性發炎 減重過快 臉部老化/`.
**[V]** = source found online (mostly abstracts), **[U]** = unverified / secondary only.

**"Too fast" for him (lean ≈68 kg):**
| Phase | 14-day trend cap | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| >84 kg (~20–23 % BF) | **0.7 %/wk** (~0.6 kg) | Garthe 2011 0.7 > 1.4 % [U numbers]; Ruiz-Castellano 2021 review [V]: 1 %/wk case lost 42.8 % as FFM |
| ≤84 kg (≤~19 %) and post-trip | **0.5 %/wk** | Helms 2020 ≤0.5 % [V]; Forbes/Hall model [V]: lean share of each kg ≈34 % at 20 kg fat → ≈46 % at 12 kg (untrained upper bound) |
| Any time, hard stop | 1 %/wk (existing [[safety-limits]]) | |
| Gallstone risk jumps | >1.5 kg/wk or >25 % of start weight lost [U]; very low fat worst (Stokes 2014 [V]: fat 19–30 % of kcal protective) | |
| Low energy availability | **EA = (intake − exercise kcal) ÷ FFM < 25 kcal/kg** → <~1,700 kcal after exercise | Ruiz-Castellano 2021 [V]; IOC REDs 2023 [V]. Male cut-off is uncertain, a direction not a line |

Fast vs slow at equal total loss: slow keeps ~1 kg more fat lost and RMR slightly better (Ashtary-Larky 2020 [V]);
regain at 3 y is the same (Purcell 2014: 71.2 vs 70.5 %). Slowing buys **muscle and recovery**, not less regain.

**His EA, computed from Garmin 2026-10-25:** activity kcal (sum of `activities.calories`, includes BMR during the
session so it overstates exercise) ≈ 590/day ISO W38–W43. Net of BMR ≈ 450. So EA ≈ (2,023 − 450)/68 ≈ **23** in the
first 13 weeks (below 25), 2,164 → ≈24, **2,520 → ≈30 (fine)**. The dry skin and hair-shedding window overlap the
low-EA weeks: fits, not proven.

Weekly EA (net of session BMR) W30–W43: **22–26, below 25 in 11 of 14 weeks**; lowest W40 (≈22, ~650 activity kcal/day).
**Checklist EA (raw activity kcal, as the review checklist computes it), W43 full review 10-25:** activity kcal/day W39 597,
W40 648, W41 601, W42 590, W43 626 → W43 2,164 − 626 = **1,538 (yellow)**; at 2,520 from 10-26 ≈ **1,890 (green)**.
**14-day slope caveat (10-24):** `healthgo check` gave −1.73 %/wk, but the window starts on the 10-11/12 water spike
(91.5 kg after eating out 10-10); 10-08 → 10-24 is −2.3 kg in 16 days ≈ −1.0 kg/wk ≈ 1.1 %/wk. Read the slope against a
longer window when the first days of the 14 are a spike.

**Side effects, threshold → signal:**
| Harm | When | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| Lean loss | faster and leaner = more | e1RM down 2 wk running, or ≥5 % below the cut's best |
| REDs / hormones (T, T3, leptin ↓, cortisol ↑) | deep or long deficit + heavy training + low carb; drug-free bodybuilders T 543–922 → 175–227 ng/dL at ~5–6 % BF (2023 review [V]), recovers in 1–3 months | RHR **falling** with cold/tired on /feel (RHR drops in deep deficits, so a low RHR is not automatically "fitter") |
| Gallstones | above | right-upper-quadrant pain >30 min after fatty food, fever, jaundice → doctor |
| Transient ALT rise | weeks 2–6 of rapid loss, settles by 16–24 wk [V] | with Hep B only HBV DNA can tell a diet bump from a flare |
| Hair (telogen effluvium) | 2–4 months after rapid loss [U timing] → **his window ≈ mid-Sep to mid-Nov 2026**; recovers 3–6 months | more hair in shower/pillow |
| Skin xerosis/itch | EFA/zinc patterns; direct rate evidence thin | [[skin-dryness-itch]]; new itch + Hep B → liver panel |
| Mood/binge | last ~2 months of contest prep; restraint +40 % | /feel hunger, irritability, waking at night |
| Bone | ∝ total loss; lifting prevents it (GLP-1 + exercise trial) | low priority for him |
| Metabolic adaptation | continuous RMR −179 vs intermittent −86 kcal/d (MATADOR) | [[evidence-metabolic-adaptation]] 6-signal rule |
| Infections | sleep <6 h, long deficit + heavy load | [[evidence-inflammation-immunity-hepb]] |
HRV has **no** evidence as an early "too fast" marker. GLP-1 trials: 25–40 % of loss is DXA lean in untrained
patients (JAMA 2024 viewpoint: sarcopenia unlikely for most); not comparable to him.
