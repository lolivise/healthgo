Darren's daily 350 g chicken breast ([[daily-diet-baseline]]) is always marinated. Given 2026-10-16.
**He makes all three at once, one per 1 kg raw breast (3 kg total), and eats a different flavour each day.**
So a cycle lasts ~8.5 days and the **average of the three is what goes into the daily baseline**.

**Names (short, so he can just say "today was 叉燒"):** **Mustard 蜜芥**, **Tikka 咖哩優格**, **Char Siu 叉燒**.

## Recipes (per 1 kg raw chicken breast)

**1. Mustard 蜜芥:** Kewpie mayonnaise 60 g, Capilano honey 60 g, Coles wholegrain mustard 60 g,
Holbrooks Worcestershire 15 g, water 60 g.

**2. Tikka 咖哩優格:** Shan Chicken Tikka mix 10 g, Shan Chicken Biryani mix 10 g, Tamar Valley Greek-style
yoghurt (plain) 200 g, water 50 g.

**3. Char Siu 叉燒:** Lee Kum Kee char siu sauce 40 g, LKK premium soy 10 g, Holbrooks Worcestershire 15 g,
Capilano honey 15 g, Laoganma chilli oil with diced chicken 25 g, MasterFoods onion powder 5 g,
MasterFoods garlic powder 5 g, Ajinomoto MSG 5 g, water 100 g.

- ⚠️ **The table is the whole marinade. He doesn't eat it all:** he **throws the leftover marinade away** before cooking
  (Darren, 2026-10-16), so only what clings to or soaks into the chicken counts. **Working estimate: ~30 % retained**
  (range 15–40 %). Nutrition-analysis rules of thumb are 15–25 % for thin marinades [M: RDN forum 15–16 %, DaVita 25 %].
  Mustard and Tikka are thick (mayo, yoghurt) and cling more, Char Siu is thin (100 g water); salt also soaks in.

  **To replace the estimate with a real number:** weigh the discarded marinade once per flavour (not a routine,
  just once). Retained % = (batch weight − discarded) ÷ batch weight. Batches are Mustard 255 g, Tikka 270 g, Char Siu 220 g.
- Biggest contributors: Mustard kcal is the mayo (433 kcal/batch) and honey (203). Tikka sodium is the Shan
  Tikka mix (salt is its first ingredient, 20,600 mg Na/100 g → 2,060 mg/batch). Char Siu sodium comes from
  char siu sauce (1,696/batch), soy (688), MSG (615) and Laoganma (442).
- **Linoleic acid (LA):** Kewpie mayo and Laoganma are soybean oil (~53 % LA). The whole marinade would be ~9 g LA per
  Mustard portion and ~3 g per Char Siu portion. At 30 % retained that is **~1.2 g/day on average**. See [[skin-dryness-itch]].

**Why it matters:** the marinades were **missing from the baseline** until 2026-10-16. Because the leftover is
discarded, they add only **~50 kcal and ~280 mg sodium a day**: within the noise of the TDEE estimate, but worth
counting. (A first version that same day counted the whole marinade, +162 kcal / +940 mg Na, and withdrew the salt advice.
That was corrected once he said he discards it.)

**How to apply:**
- Count **+50 kcal / +3 g fat / +280 mg Na per day** on top of the plain food. `config/plan.json` adds +49 to every intake entry
  back to 2026-07-15 (1,974 → 2,023, 2,030 → 2,079, 2,115 → 2,164). This assumes he has used them since the cut began,
  which he hasn't confirmed.
- If he weighs the discarded marinade, recompute the retained table and the plan.json numbers from the real %.
- If he changes a recipe or drops a flavour, recompute that row and the average (the script is simple: grams × label ÷ 100).
- On a day-level question (e.g. a salty day or a scale jump), the flavour matters: Mustard is the kcal-heavy one,
  Char Siu and Tikka are the salty ones.
- To cut kcal later without changing the routine, Mustard's mayo + honey is the lever (60 g mayo ≈ 145 kcal per 350 g
  in the whole marinade, ~45 kcal eaten).
