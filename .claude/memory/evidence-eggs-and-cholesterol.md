Darren asked (2026-10-26) how many eggs a day are fine, benefits and drawbacks. **[V]** = abstract/full text checked,
**[S]** = memory or secondary only. Flags name what is weak about the evidence.

## Old belief → now
**"Eggs raise cholesterol, max ~3 a week / 300 mg cholesterol a day"** → the 300 mg cap was dropped (US DGA 2015) [S];
AHA 2020 sets no cholesterol number, judges the whole diet (Carson, PMID 31838890) [V]. Saturated fat predicts LDL,
egg cholesterol doesn't (crossover n=61, PMID 40339906) [V]. **The opposite myth is wrong too**: eggs are not "free" —
RCTs show a small LDL rise on average, and data above 1/day is thin.

## Evidence
| Question | Answer | Key source | Flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heart / death, ≤1 egg/day | **no harm** | Drouin-Chartier 2020 BMJ (PMID 32132002) [V]: 215k + MA 1.7M, per egg/d RR 0.98 (0.93–1.03); PURE, 50 countries, ≥7/wk HR 1.04 death, 0.92 CVD (PMID 31965140) [V]; China Kadoorie daily egg CVD HR 0.89 (PMID 29785957) [V] | observational, FFQ |
| Harm signal | small, US only | Zhong 2019 JAMA (PMID 30874756) [V]: +½ egg/d CVD HR 1.06, death 1.08; Zhao 2022 MA (PMID 35360933) [V]: US 1.08, Europe borderline, **Asia null** | I² 80 %; confounded (bacon, butter, higher BMI, red meat) |
| LDL in trials | +~7 mg/dL average, HDL up too | umbrella of 14 MAs (PMID 39934049) [V]: LDL +7.4 (5.8–9.0), quality "critically low"; 66-RCT MA: LDL:HDL ratio unchanged (PMID 32524644) [V] | short trials, mixed diets |
| 2 eggs/day | no LDL change in most | Eggant RCT n=105, 8 wk: no LDL/apoB change (PMID 36678239) [V]; hyperlipidaemia n=45: +4.2 mg/dL, p=0.32 (PMID 40957619) [V] | small; funding not checked |
| Type 2 diabetes | US yes, Asia no | +1 egg/d: US RR 1.18, Europe 0.99, Asia 0.82 (PMID 32453379) [V]; RCTs null | observational |
| Liver / Hep B | **no data** | nothing egg-specific on fatty liver or HBV | — |
| Muscle | whole egg ≥ white | van Vliet 2017 (PMID 28978542) [V]: whole egg more MPS post-lifting (n=10); 3 whole vs 6 whites 12 wk, more fat loss + strength (Bagheri 2021, PMID 33306586, n=30) [V] | tiny n; whey still ranks first (PMID 42862730) |

## Who should limit (≤ ~7/wk, doctor's call)
High LDL or familial hypercholesterolaemia, established heart disease, type 2 diabetes (US data). Some people are
"hyper-responders" whose LDL jumps on eggs; no reliable rate found. Only a blood test shows which you are.

## Guidelines
AHA 2020: no cholesterol target, ~1 egg/day fine for healthy people [S for the egg line]. Heart Foundation Australia
2019: up to 7/wk is fine [S, site unreachable]. Nordic NNR 2023: ≤1/day not harmful [V]. Taiwan HPA: not retrieved.

## How it applies to Darren (2026-10-26)

change; his diet is low in saturated fat (chicken breast, olive oil, oats, walnuts), the context where eggs don't raise
LDL; Asian cohorts null; no diabetes. **No plan change.** The one open fact is his own LDL/apoB: read it at the next blood
test; if LDL is high, cut to ~1/day and recheck. Fried eggs: the oil and side meats matter more than the egg.
