Checked 2026-11-19 when he restarted finasteride 1.25 mg/day ([[health-conditions]]). Full notes with links:
`research/medical/notes/finasteride/finasteride_check.md`. [V] = abstract or label read.

| Topic | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Works for male pattern hair loss | +107 hairs vs placebo at 1 y, +138 at 2 y (5.1 cm² vertex); label +277 at 5 y; judge from 3–6 mo, counts significant at 6 and 12 mo | Kaufman 1998, PMID 9777765, 2 RCTs n=1,553 [V]; Mella 2010 SR, PMID 20956649, 12 RCTs n=3,927 [V] |
| Dose | DHT −72 % at 1 and 5 mg, −71 % at 0.2 mg (plateau); hair 1 ≈ 5 mg > 0.2 mg. 1.25 mg never tested but on the plateau; quartered-tablet accuracy unknown | Drake 1999, PMID 10495374, n=249, 42 d [V]; Roberts 1999, PMID 10495375 [V] |
| Stopping | label: effect reverses within 12 mo; finasteride → placebo men lost the gain by month 24; DHT back to baseline ~2 wk | Propecia/Proscar labels [V] |
| Restarting | no data on a restart after a break. Late starters (placebo → drug, n=425) ended +56 hairs above baseline vs +91 for continuous use | Propecia label [V] |
| Telogen effluvium | no data; indication is pattern hair loss only | — |
| Sexual AE | 12 mo: any 3.8 % vs 2.1 % (p=0.04), ED 1.3 vs 0.7, libido 1.8 vs 1.3; ED RR 2.22 (1.03–4.78). **He had lower libido + fewer morning erections on his first course** | label n=945 vs 934 [V]; Mella 2010 [V] |
| Mood | conflicting: Swedish cohort HR 1.61 (PMID 36547981); UK Biobank null (PMID 41330881); 2026 MA HR 1.07 (0.76–1.51, PMID 42890027). EMA PRAC 2025 lists suicidal ideation; patient card for 1 mg | [V] |
| Persistent symptoms after stopping | case series/surveys only (e.g. PMID 42074750); PCPT follow-up null (PMID 42628582): unsettled, low quality | [V] |
| Liver / entecavir | finasteride: liver CYP3A4, label "exercise caution" with abnormal liver function, doesn't affect CYP. Entecavir: not a CYP substrate, 62–73 % renal unchanged → no shared pathway. No Hep B data. LiverTox not read at source | labels [V] |
| PSA | 1 mg: 0.7 → 0.5 ng/mL at 12 mo; 5 mg ≈ −50 %; any confirmed rise from nadir needs work-up | label [V] |
| Handling | women pregnant or possibly pregnant must not handle crushed/broken tablets (male fetus genitalia); semen exposure tiny (≤1.52 ng/mL). **He splits 5 mg tablets → broken tablets** | label 5.1 [V] |
| Muscle | no RCT in trained men; indirect: dutasteride + T (Bhasin 2012, PMID 22396515, n=139) and finasteride 5 mg + T (Borst 2014, PMID 24326421, n=60 older men) didn't blunt FFM/strength gains | [V] |
| Testosterone | finasteride blocks T → DHT, it does **not** lower T: label (finasteride 5 mg BPH generic) "median circulating testosterone increased by approximately 10–20 % but remained within the physiologic range"; 1 mg phase III review: DHT −68.4 %, T +9.1 % median at 1 y (McClellan & Markham 1999, Drugs; review, not read in full) | label [V] (drugcentral, 11-19); review [S] |
| Creatine | DHT +56 % (van der Merwe 2009, n=20, unreplicated); hair RCT null (Lak 2025) → [[evidence-supplements]] | [V] |

**For him:** restart is evidence-backed for pattern loss; stopping ~6 mo ago may have contributed to the thinning but can't be
separated from telogen effluvium ([[evidence-rapid-loss-harms]]). Judge hair at 6 and 12 mo, not weeks. Prior sexual AE is
his own best predictor (inference); if it returns or mood drops → prescribing doctor. Tell labs/doctors (PSA). Doctor cleared
it with entecavir; ALT at the usual 6-monthly bloodwork.
