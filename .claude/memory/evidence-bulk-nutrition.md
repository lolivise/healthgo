**Decided for Darren (Opus, 2026-10-28); numbers = arithmetic on the evidence, not a tested diet:**
1. **Order:** energy (small surplus) → protein → fat floor and fat *type* → carbs fill the rest → timing (tie-breaker).
   Steps 1–2 are trial-based; the fat floor is convention; the carb share has no muscle effect either way.
2. **Protein stays ~165 g (≈2.0 g/kg at 82 kg, 20–25 % kcal). Don't raise it in the bulk**; extra kcal go to carbs + fat.
3. **Fat 0.9–1.0 g/kg (~75–85 g, ~25–28 % kcal)**, floor 0.7 g/kg unchanged ([[safety-limits]]). **SFA ≤10 % kcal
   (~30 g)**: guideline cut-off, backed by the LDL and liver-fat trials. Added fat = olive/canola oil, nuts, fatty fish
   2–3×/wk, eggs; not butter, cream, coconut/palm oil, fried/baked snacks.
4. **Carbs = the rest, ~4–5 g/kg (≈330–395 g, ~50–53 %)**: rice, potato, oats, fruit, dairy; fibre ≥25–29 g
   (Reynolds 2019 Lancet, PMID 30638909, dose-response in cohorts + RCTs). No keto, no low-carb bulk.
5. **Worked splits (82 kg):** 2,700 kcal = P 165 / F 80 / C 330 g · 3,000 kcal = P 165 / F 85 / C 395 g.
6. **Pattern:** 4 meals of ~35–45 g protein over a ≥12 h eating day (his current shape fits; entecavir window intact);
   surplus added inside meals, **not as extra snacks**; no 16:8 on a bulk. Yogurt at dinner already = pre-sleep protein.
7. **Prevent list, ranked:** (a) overshooting the surplus: gain 0.2–0.4 kg/wk, and if weight rises while e1RM doesn't,
   the surplus is buying fat; (b) SFA as the surplus vehicle; (c) liquid sugar (手搖飲, juice); (d) fast-eaten
   energy-dense processed food as the "easy" kcal; (e) letting steps/cardio fall: keep the treadmill; (f) heavy drinking
   after training; (g) a long bulk with a big surplus followed by a long cut.
8. **Labs for the bulk:** liver panel + lipids (LDL, TG, ideally apoB) at his twice-yearly bloodwork. Because entecavir is
   dosed by eGFR and both meat and creatine raise creatinine: **ask his doctor** whether to draw creatinine fasted and add
   cystatin C. Hep B + extra liver fat is ❓ unresolved, so liver questions go to the doctor ([[health-conditions]]).

**Unresolved:** protein dose inside a lifting surplus; carb-vs-fat split at equal kcal in a surplus (no trial); whether a
+5–10 % surplus raises liver fat in a lean trained man (all trials +750–1,000 kcal, untrained/overweight); whether lifting
(not running) protects against overfeeding; alcohol below binge dose.
