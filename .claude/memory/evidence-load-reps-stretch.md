Darren asked (2026-10-21, voice note, interpreted): is heavy weight a must, or do lighter 12–15-rep sets grow muscle
equally because growth comes from hard contractions and stretch? **[V]** = checked online, **[M]** = model memory.
Supersedes the [M] Load/Failure/Long-length rows of [[evidence-hypertrophy-training]].

| Question | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Load | Currier 2023 BJSM Bayesian network meta-analysis, 119 hypertrophy / 178 strength studies [V]: **all prescriptions gave comparable hypertrophy**; >80 % 1RM best for strength; multi-set ranked top for size | strong |
| Light load needs effort | Lasevicius 2022 [V]: 30 % 1RM to failure = 80 % 1RM for quad CSA (+7.8 vs +8.1 %); 30 % stopped short of failure only +2.8 % (ns); strength ~2× with heavy. Lasevicius 2018 [V via summary]: 20 % 1RM lagged 40–80 % | moderate |
| Failure | Refalo 2023 Sports Med, 15 studies [V]: failure vs non-failure trivial (ES 0.19); momentary failure no edge (0.12, ns); non-linear. Robinson 2024 [V, in cut file]: growth improves as sets get closer to failure. 2026 MA, 20 RCTs (42410632) [V abstract]: hypertrophy ns; **strength favours non-failure** (SMD 0.24). Default 1–2 RIR; failure only on safe machine/isolation sets | moderate |
| Stretch / long length | Wolf 2025 Sports Med Health Sci review [V]: long-length training ≥ short, maybe more fascicle/size gain, data mixed. Wolf 2025 PeerJ, 30 trained, within-arm 8 wk [V]: lengthened partials = full ROM (BF 0.16–0.3 favour null). Gschneidner 2025, 297 people, 12 wk [V via summary]: ES ≈ −0.03. → **Train through the stretched position; extra "stretch-only" tricks add little** | moderate |
| Glutes | Plotkin 2023 Front Physiol MRI, untrained, 9 wk [V]: hip thrust ≈ back squat for glute growth despite higher hip-thrust EMG; squat grew quads/adductors more. **EMG/"feel" does not predict growth** | low–moderate |
| "Number of contractions" | Hypertrophy tracks **hard sets** (sets ending ~0–3 RIR) and weekly volume (Pelland 2024/25 [V, in cut file]), not raw rep count. Easy reps far from failure count for little | strong (consensus) |

## Verdict (Opus, 2026-10-21)
- **The "you don't need heavy" camp is right for muscle size**, with a condition people drop: the set must end
  close to failure. 12–15 reps at a load where rep 15 is a real struggle ≈ 6–8 heavy reps for growth.
- **Heavy loads win for strength**, and strength is his scoreboard during the cut ([[performance-first]]). So keep at
  least the main presses/pulls in 6–12 and use 12–20 for isolation, joints and the right shoulder.
- "Stretch matters" = use full ROM with control at the bottom; it is not a separate magic stimulus.
- His log already fits this: lateral raise 12 × 15, triceps 20 × 15, curl 12.5 × 20, bench 50 × 10 ([[training-routine]]).
  Light sets only count if they reach ~1–2 RIR; record RIR or at least never stop "with plenty left".
- No squat/deadlift, and **no hip thrust either** (Darren, 2026-10-21: he wants functional glutes for walking and
  running, not glute size). His current leg day covers it: deep leg press (glute max, 6 sets, 200 kg × 10 on
  10-20), standing hip abduction (glute med, 2 sets), plus ~9,500 steps/day. ⛔ `CABLE_KICKBACK` in Garmin is his
  **triceps** kickback on Shoulder Day, not a glute move (corrected 2026-10-21). Don't add glute exercises unless he asks; just keep those sets
  near failure.
