**Research must end in action, and must spot hype.** Darren, 2026-10-26: "the main goal is after the knowledge
gathering, what may it change to my plan, and if there is a suggestion for me to try something" — and: "the supplements
that people said is good for your body, that sometime don't have strong scientific proven or a scientific experiment
with concerning lab setup or data reading. that need to spot." He sees eggs/cholesterol as the model case: a belief held
for decades on misleading evidence, hard to shift once accepted.

**Why:** knowledge that doesn't change what he does is wasted tokens, and nutrition/supplement claims are where weak
studies (tiny n, surrogate markers, industry funding, animal data, conclusions stronger than the numbers) do the most damage.

**How to apply:**
- Every research answer and every `/knowledge-review` reply leads with **對你計畫的影響** (or "no change, because…"),
  then **值得一試**, then **hype spotted** with what is wrong with the evidence.
- Evidence files carry a flags column and one `Old belief → now` row per overturned myth ([[evidence-eggs-and-cholesterol]]).
- "Worth trying" never bypasses Hep B: a new supplement is only ever "ask your doctor" ([[health-conditions]], [[supplements]]).
  Food, training and habit changes can be proposed directly.
- Nutrition (foods + supplements) is a quarterly sector since 2026-10-26 (`research/README.md`).
